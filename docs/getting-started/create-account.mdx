Archon uses Supabase for authentication. Your session is stored securely in the iOS Keychain, so you stay signed in across app launches.

## Sign up with email

<Steps>
  <Step title="Tap Sign Up">
    On the login screen, tap **Sign Up** to create a new account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your email and password">
    Provide a valid email address and choose a secure password.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify your email">
    Check your inbox for a verification email from Supabase. Tap the link to confirm your account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="You're in">
    Archon will take you to the onboarding flow, then to your project dashboard.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Sign in with Apple

<Steps>
  <Step title="Tap Sign in with Apple">
    On the login screen, tap the **Sign in with Apple** button.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm with Face ID or Touch ID">
    Authenticate using your device's biometric or passcode.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your email visibility">
    Apple will ask whether to share your real email or use a private relay address. Either works with Archon.
  </Step>
  <Step title="You're in">
    Archon completes authentication and takes you to your dashboard.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Sign in with Apple is the recommended option. It requires no password management and uses Apple's secure authentication flow.
</Note>

## Session management

- Sessions are stored in the **iOS Keychain** and persist across app launches.
- If your session expires, Archon will prompt you to sign in again.
- You can sign out from **Settings → Account → Sign Out**.
