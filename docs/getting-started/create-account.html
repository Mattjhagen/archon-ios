Create Your Account

Getting Started ~1 min

Archon supports two sign-in methods: Apple Sign In (recommended) and email + password.

Option A: Apple Sign In (recommended)

Fastest and most secure

Tap Sign in with Apple on the onboarding screen. Your Apple ID handles authentication — Archon never sees your password. Sessions are stored in your device's Keychain.

  • → Tap Sign in with Apple
  • → Authenticate with Face ID, Touch ID, or your passcode
  • → Choose whether to share your email or use a private relay address
  • → Done — you're signed in

Option B: Email + Password

Traditional sign-up

Enter your email and create a password. You'll receive a verification email to confirm your account.

  • → Tap Create Account
  • → Enter your email and a password (8+ characters)
  • → Check your inbox for a verification link
  • → Tap the link to verify — then sign in

What you get

Both methods give you access to:

  • → Free starter credits — enough for your first few projects
  • → Project syncing — your projects are saved to the cloud
  • → AI model selection — choose GPT-4o, Claude, Gemini, or Ollama
  • → Learning Center — interactive coding lessons with progress tracking
