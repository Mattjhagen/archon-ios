Install Archon

Getting Started ~2 min

Archon is an AI-powered app builder available on iOS and Android. Download it, sign in, and start building in minutes.

iOS — TestFlight Beta

Join via TestFlight

Install Apple's TestFlight app from the App Store, then tap the link below to join the Archon beta.

Join iOS TestFlight Beta →
  • → iPhone or iPad with iOS 17 or later
  • → An Apple ID with App Store access
  • → TestFlight app installed

Android — Google Play

Install from Google Play

Find Archon on the Google Play Store and tap Install.

Get it on Google Play →
  • → Android device with Android 8.0 or later
  • → A Google account with Play Store access
  • → An internet connection (for AI features)

What's next?

After installing, you'll go through a quick onboarding flow. Next step:
