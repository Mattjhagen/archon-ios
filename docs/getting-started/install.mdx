## System requirements

- iPhone running **iOS 17** or later
- Internet connection (for AI builds and project sync)
- An Apple ID or email address for account creation

## Installation

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the App Store">
    Tap the **App Store** icon on your home screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Search for Archon">
    Search for **"Archon"** or visit the direct link from the Archon website.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Download and install">
    Tap **Get** to download Archon. The app is free to download.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Archon">
    Tap **Open** or find Archon on your home screen.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Archon also supports TestFlight for beta builds. If you have a TestFlight invitation link, open it on your iPhone to install the beta version. See [TestFlight setup](/getting-started/install#testflight-beta) below.
</Note>

## TestFlight beta

<Steps>
  <Step title="Get the TestFlight link">
    Request an invitation from the Archon team or check the [GitHub releases page](https://github.com/Mattjhagen/archon-ios/releases).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Install TestFlight">
    Download the **TestFlight** app from the App Store if you don't already have it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Accept the invitation">
    Open the invitation link on your iPhone. TestFlight will prompt you to install Archon.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Install the beta">
    Tap **Install** inside TestFlight. Beta updates will appear automatically when new builds are available.
  </Step>
</Steps>
