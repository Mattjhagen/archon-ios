Welcome to Archon. Describe the app you want in plain language, and an AI agent builds it for you step by step. Browse the generated code, preview it live, and manage all your projects from one place.

</Frame>

## What you can do with Archon

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Describe and build" icon="wand-magic-sparkles" href="/getting-started/first-project">
    Tell Archon what you want in chat. The AI builds it, shows progress in a task timeline, and delivers generated code you can browse and edit.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Browse and edit code" icon="code" href="/features/code-browser">
    Navigate the generated file tree, read syntax-highlighted source in Swift, JavaScript, TypeScript, HTML, CSS, or JSON, and make edits.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Preview live" icon="play" href="/features/live-preview">
    See your app running in a live WKWebView preview. Go full screen to test the experience before you share it.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Manage projects" icon="folder-open" href="/features/projects">
    Create, search, and organize projects. Pick up where you left off with full conversation history and generated files.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Quick start

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install Archon">
    Download Archon from the App Store. Requires iOS 17 or later.
    [Install Archon →](/getting-started/install)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Sign in with your email or use Apple Sign In. Your sessions are stored securely in the iOS Keychain.
    [Create an account →](/getting-started/create-account)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build your first app">
    Open the Builder tab, describe what you want, and watch Archon build it in real time.
    [Build your first project →](/getting-started/first-project)
  </Step>
</Steps>
